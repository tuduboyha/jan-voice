About Jan Voice

Every Issue Matters. Every Voice Counts.

Jan Voice is a platform built for one purpose: to let citizens raise real social issues and debate them honestly, in the open, with structure instead of noise.

Most comment sections collapse into shouting matches with no clear sides and no way to see where the conversation actually stands. Jan Voice fixes that by asking one simple question before anyone can comment: are you Support or Oppose ? That single choice keeps every discussion organized into two clear, courtroom-style columns, backed by live statistics showing exactly how the country is split.

We believe democracy works best when disagreement is visible, structured, and evidence-based — not hidden behind algorithmic feeds or drowned out by the loudest voice in the room.

What we stand for

  • Every opinion deserves to be heard and respected
  • Evidence-based discussion over rhetoric
  • Zero tolerance for hate speech, abuse, or fake news
  • Full transparency in how issues are moderated
