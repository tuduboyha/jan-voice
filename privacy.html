Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when creating an account (username, email, password), when posting content (issues, comments, opinions), and basic technical data used for security.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to operate the platform: authenticating you, displaying your posts and profile, sending you notifications, and moderating content for community safety.

3. What We Never Do

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Passwords are managed entirely by Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashing and are never visible to us.

4. Your Choices

You can post anonymously on any issue, update or delete your profile information from your dashboard, and request account deletion by contacting us.

5. Data Storage

Application data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL). Sessions are managed via secure tokens handled entirely by Supabase Auth in your browser.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.
